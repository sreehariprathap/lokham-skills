# Posting plan

Consistency beats bursts. A plan the person keeps for 30 days beats a perfect plan they drop in 3.

## Default plan (adjust to their time)
- **1-3 clips a day**, same time slots each day (e.g. 12:00 and 19:00 local time).
- Post each clip to **Shorts, Reels and TikTok**, with captions edited per platform (don't leave watermarks of other apps).
- **Batch**: one session cuts a week of clips; posting then takes minutes a day.
- After 2 weeks, look at the tracking sheet: which hooks and clip types got the most views at 24 h? Make more of those.

## Tracking sheet
Copy `assets/tracking.csv` and fill one row per post:
date, platform, clip file, hook, source footage / campaign, link, views 24 h, views 7 d, submitted to campaign (y/n), paid ($).

## Weekly check (5 minutes)
- Top 3 clips by 24 h views: what do their hooks have in common?
- Any rejected submissions? Re-read the brief.
- Campaign budgets: still open?
