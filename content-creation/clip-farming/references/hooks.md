# Hooks and captions for gaming clips

The viewer decides in about one second. Put the strongest frame and one short line first.

## Cutting
- Start 1-2 seconds before the payoff, never at the calm lead-up. If the payoff needs setup, show the payoff first
  as a flash (0.5 s), then rewind: "wait for it".
- End right after the reaction. A clip that ends a beat early gets rewatched.
- 10-30 s for most clips. Longer only if the story needs it.

## Hook line formulas (max ~6 words, on screen at 0 s)
- Disbelief: "HE DID WHAT?!", "NO WAY THIS WORKED"
- Rarity: "1 IN A MILLION GTA 6 MOMENT", "NOBODY HAS SEEN THIS YET"
- Stakes: "5 STARS IN 10 SECONDS", "LAST LIFE. 1 HP."
- Curiosity: "WATCH THE CAR ON THE LEFT", "WAIT FOR THE NPC"
- Comedy: "GTA 6 NPCS ARE UNHINGED"
Keep it true to the clip. Clickbait that the clip doesn't pay off kills retention and gets clips rejected.

## Captions
- Caption any speech, 2-4 words at a time, high contrast, middle of the screen (keep clear of the top 9% and the
  bottom 30% where the app's buttons and text sit, and of the right-hand button column).
- Highlight 1 key word per line in a colour.

## Titles / post captions
Hook + one line of context + 3-5 specific hashtags, e.g.
"This GTA 6 police chase ended in the worst way 💀 #gta6 #gta6clips #gaming #gtaonline".
Follow the campaign's required tags and mentions exactly.
