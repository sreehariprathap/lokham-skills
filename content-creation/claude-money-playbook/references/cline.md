# Cline: websites for local shops
Repo: github.com/cline/cline · Licence: Apache-2.0 · Autonomous coding agent for VS Code (and CLI/SDK).

## Quickstart (docs "Installing Cline", re-check)
1. In VS Code open Extensions and search "Cline".
2. Install it and open it from the sidebar.
3. Choose a provider (Claude is a common choice) or your own API key.
4. Describe the website in plain words and review what it writes.

## What to sell, and to whom
Small business websites, booking pages and simple internal tools. Buyers: local shops, clinics, cafes, trades with no site or a very old one.

## Demo idea (suggestion)
Pick a local business with a poor site, build a one-page version in an afternoon, and send them the screenshot and link.

## Rough money
About $2,000-8,000 for a small site. websitesetup.org (fetched): freelancer $2,000-7,600, agency $5,000-15,000. A search snippet gave a similar picture. Hourly rates for AI developers on Upwork ($30-150) were seen in a snippet only.

## Watch out for
You are responsible for what ships: check forms, mobile layout, accessibility and who owns the domain and hosting. Agree who owns the code.
