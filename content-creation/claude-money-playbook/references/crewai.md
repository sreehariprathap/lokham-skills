# CrewAI: a small AI team for a business
Repo: github.com/crewAIInc/crewAI · Licence: MIT · Framework for role-playing, collaborating AI agents.

## Quickstart (README "Getting Started", re-check)
1. `uv tool install crewai`
2. `crewai create crew <name>`
3. `crewai install`, then `crewai run`
Define each agent's role, goal and tasks in the generated config.

## What to sell, and to whom
Multi-step workflows: lead research, weekly reports, content pipelines. Buyers: marketing agencies and small firms.

## Demo idea (suggestion)
A 3-agent crew (Researcher, Writer, Reviewer) that turns a company's website into a one-page competitor brief.

## Rough money
Digital Agency Network (USD): setup $2,500-15,000, small-business retainers under $2,000 a month, mid-market $3,000-6,000 a month. Sandlabs (AUD, not converted): simple automation A$5K-15K, multi-agent systems A$40K-80K. They disagree, so use about $2,500-15,000 for small clients.

## Watch out for
Agents can be wrong or loop. Add a human review step and a cost cap before delivering to a client.
