# AnythingLLM: private AI over company documents
Repo: github.com/Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm · Licence: MIT · Local-first "chat with your documents" app.

## Quickstart (README, re-check)
1. Download the Desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux).
2. Drag and drop documents into a workspace.
3. Chat and read the cited sources.
Multi-user, white-label and the website chat widget are Docker-only features.

## What to sell, and to whom
A private "ask our documents" assistant: policies, manuals, contracts, FAQs. Buyers: law firms, clinics, accountants, small teams with lots of paperwork.

## Demo idea (suggestion)
Load a sample policy PDF, ask "what is our refund policy?" and show the answer with its source.

## Rough money
SOURCES DISAGREE. About $1,500+ for a simple private setup (one Upwork posting at $1,750 fixed price). Bespoke document Q&A builds are far higher: RaftLabs quotes $12K-30K with $400-800 a month run cost, but that is a custom build, not a tool setup. Treat anything above $1,500 as "depends on scope".

## Watch out for
Confidential documents: agree where data lives, who can access it, and which AI model processes it.
