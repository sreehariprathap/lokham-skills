# Postiz: done-for-you social media posting
Repo: github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app · Licence: AGPL-3.0 · Social media scheduling with AI helpers. Docs: docs.postiz.com/quickstart

## Quickstart (docs, re-check)
1. Sign up on Postiz Cloud, or self-host and register.
2. Add Channel and connect each social account.
3. Write the post, pick the time, schedule it.
Self-hosting means creating your own developer app on each platform; Meta, YouTube and TikTok approvals can take weeks.

## What to sell, and to whom
A month of planned posts for a small business: restaurants, gyms, real-estate agents, salons.

## Demo idea (suggestion)
Make a 30-day calendar for one business (themes, captions, image briefs) and show a week scheduled.

## Rough money
About $500-1,500 per month per client. Overlap of SocialBu ($500-1,500 for small business) and Sprout Social ($500-5,000 for basic programs). Bark averages (snippet) were similar.

## Watch out for
AGPL: if you host a modified Postiz for clients you must publish your changes. Using Postiz Cloud or the unmodified app and selling the service is fine. Get the client's written OK to post as them.
