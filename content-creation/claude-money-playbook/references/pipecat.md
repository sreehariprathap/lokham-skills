# Pipecat: AI phone receptionist
Repo: github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat · Licence: BSD-2-Clause · Open-source framework for voice agents and realtime AI.

## Quickstart (README "Getting started", 2026-11-24, re-check)
1. Install uv (`curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh`).
2. `uv tool install "pipecat-ai[cli]"`
3. `pipecat init` and answer the questions to get a working bot.
4. Add a phone number through a telephony provider (see the generated project's docs).

## What to sell, and to whom
An AI receptionist that answers calls, answers common questions (hours, prices) and books jobs. Buyers: dental clinics, salons, plumbers and other home-service businesses that miss calls.

## Demo idea (suggestion, not from the project)
Build a bot for one real local business: give it their opening hours, services and a booking rule, call it yourself, record the call as your pitch.

## Rough money
About $700-1,500 setup plus a monthly fee. WEAK source: one UK agency's published rates (about GBP 550-1,200 setup plus GBP 350-750 a month). Retell's agency guide says to price the build separately but gives no amounts. Say "from a few hundred dollars up" if challenged.

## Watch out for
Phone calls have call-recording and consent rules that vary by country and state. Tell the client what is recorded.
