# First-client plan (keep it small)
1. Pick ONE tool and ONE type of business you understand.
2. Build a small demo for a real local business (see each tool's demo idea).
3. Write a one-line offer: "I'll [result] for [business type] for $X setup and $Y a month."
4. Message 10 businesses with the demo (templates in outreach-templates.md).
5. Deliver, ask for one short testimonial, then raise the price for the next client.
6. Track your costs (API usage, tools, your hours) so you know your real margin.
7. Write down what the client must provide and what you are NOT responsible for. Keep it in a simple one-page agreement.
Not legal or financial advice.
