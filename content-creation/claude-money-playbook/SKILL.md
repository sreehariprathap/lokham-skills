# Claude Money Playbook (Million Ways to Make Money with Claude, episode 1)

From Just.intelligence.Things, written 2026-11-24. Prices here are ROUGH ranges published by agencies and freelancers. They are not promises or income claims. Say so at the start and again when you quote a number.

## How to use this skill
1. Ask the user three things: what they can already do (code, design, social media, sales, none), how many hours a week they have, and which kind of business they would enjoy helping (clinics, local shops, restaurants, agencies, firms).
2. Recommend ONE tool from the table, using their answers. Do not tour all seven unless asked.
3. Read that tool's file in `references/` and walk the user through its quickstart. Check the project's current README before running commands, they change.
4. Use `references/first-client-plan.md` to build a small demo, a simple offer and a price.
5. Use `references/outreach-templates.md` to draft the first messages.
6. Check `references/pricing-and-sources.md` before quoting any price, and say how solid the source is.

## Honesty rules
- Never tell the user they will earn a specific amount. Income depends on their clients, skills and results.
- If a price source is weak (Pipecat, AnythingLLM, Firecrawl freelance rates), say so.
- AGPL tools (Postiz, Firecrawl): if you modify the software and let clients use it over a network you must share your changes. Selling the service itself is fine. Suggest asking a lawyer before building a hosted product on them.
- Do not copy other creators' scripts or footage. Build the user's own demos.
- Do not help build spam, fake reviews or anything that breaks a platform's rules.
